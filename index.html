CORE PILLARS

Our Guiding Coordinates

We stand on the principle that the stars belong to everyone. Here is how we are mapping the journey to democratized spaceflight.

Equal Opportunity Access

Eliminating nationalistic, financial, and geopolitical hurdles. If you qualify physically, mentally, and technically, you have a flight pathway.

Open-Border Cooperation

Leveraging decentralized international partnerships to pool training resources, simulators, and space launches without territorial gatekeeping.

Open Source Aerospace

Developing shared, open-source training curricula, safety parameters, and architectural frameworks to lower launch costs for all of humanity.

TRAINING & MISSION TIMELINE

The Astronaut Journey

Our decentralized framework transforms qualified citizen candidates into mission-ready spacefarers through a structured five-step path.

PHASE 01

Registration & Skill Profiling

Create your cosmic profile, log your scientific/technical expertise, and complete initial online cognitive assessments.

PHASE 02

Regional Physical Conditioning

Undergo localized physical screening, endurance training, and scuba-based zero-gravity simulation modules with regional partner facilities.

PHASE 03

Analogue Missions & Simulation

Participate in multi-week isolation simulations at analogue Martian bases, learning team dynamics, resource conservation, and extravehicular operations.

PHASE 04

Suborbital Test Flights

Graduate to suborbital flights and microgravity flight profiles, solidifying rocket integration, G-force tolerance, and instrument management.

PHASE 05

Orbital Deployment & Beyond

Assign to joint scientific research crews on collaborative space stations, deploying global civilian science projects into Earth's orbit.
